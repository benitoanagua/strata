Add 16 editorial components for news and wiki sites (meta, tag-list, section-header, author-box, share, comment, comment-thread, article-header, entry, quote, ticker, filter-bar, index-list, ranked-list, gallery, page-layout), taxonomy hue tokens (`tagHue`, `data-tag-hues="mono"` opt-out), a `hue`/`href` API on `strata-tag`, and editorial prose styles (cite, drop cap, mark, zebra tables).

Also adds nine article-navigation components for wiki pages: toc (scroll-spy, list/rail), reading-time, reading-progress, back-to-top, cite, references, hatnote, article-nav, heading-anchors.
